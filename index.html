The Boat

Premium Comfort,
Built for Adventure

Our luxury wake boat is fully equipped for a day of thrills or a night of relaxation — no detail overlooked.

  • Plush tan leather seating throughout — room for up to 15 guests
  • Wakeboard tower with premium speakers for the perfect soundtrack
